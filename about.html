• Our Story

A Vision for Energy
Independence

MO Solar Energy Ltd is a renewable energy company focused on delivering reliable, high-performance solar power solutions to homes, businesses, and institutions across Nigeria and Africa. We specialize in designing and deploying off-grid and hybrid energy systems that eliminate dependence on unreliable grid supply and significantly reduce the cost and complexity of diesel-powered electricity.

With a growing portfolio of installations across residential, commercial, and institutional sectors, we have developed a strong reputation for delivering systems that are not only functional but optimized for performance, durability, and scalability. Beyond individual projects, we are committed to advancing energy access at scale through mini-grid solutions in partnership with government and private sector stakeholders.

Our Vision

To be a leading provider of renewable energy solutions in Africa, driving the transition to clean, dependable power and enabling economic and social development.

Our Mission

To deliver innovative, high-quality solar and energy solutions that empower homes, businesses, and communities with reliable, cost-effective, and sustainable power.
